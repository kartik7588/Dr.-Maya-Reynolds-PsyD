# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js and Tailwind CSS, required by the assignment. Deployment target is a public Vercel-compatible build.

## Users

The primary visitor is an adult in Santa Monica or elsewhere in California who appears capable and high-functioning but feels privately overwhelmed by anxiety, panic, trauma, burnout, perfectionism, or sustained internal pressure. They need to understand whether Dr. Maya Reynolds' approach and session options feel like a good fit, then take a low-friction next step.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, warm, locally relevant therapy practice homepage for fictional psychologist Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD. The page should help prospective clients feel understood, explain Maya's specialties and approach in plain language, and make it easy to inquire about in-person or secure telehealth therapy.

## Positioning

Maya combines practical, evidence-based tools with careful depth-oriented work for thoughtful, high-achieving adults whose outward capability often hides anxiety, trauma responses, or burnout.

## Operating Context

This is an internship evaluation deliverable. The final redesign must retain the source Conejo Valley Family Counseling homepage's recognizable section order, layout hierarchy, and responsive behavior while replacing its theme, images, and copy. Evaluation emphasizes cloning accuracy, design judgment, profile-grounded copy and imagery, a custom office section, and a five-minute client-facing walkthrough.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive homepage for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Required framework: Next.js with Tailwind CSS.
- Preserve the source homepage's overall composition and content rhythm.
- Include profile-derived hero, empathy statement, client focus, expertise list, approach, three core services, custom office section, FAQ, consultation CTA, and footer.
- Local SEO must naturally reference therapy in Santa Monica and telehealth across California.
- In-person sessions are offered at 123th Street 45 W, Santa Monica, CA 90401; secure telehealth is offered to clients located in California.
- Do not invent testimonials, outcomes, insurance participation, pricing, phone numbers, email addresses, or extra credentials.

## Brand Commitments

- Practice identity: Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD, Licensed Clinical Psychologist (fictional profile supplied for the assignment).
- Voice: warm, grounded, respectful, clear, collaborative, and emotionally intelligent; never clinical or sentimental for effect.
- Supplied portrait and two supplied office photographs are authoritative assets and must be used intentionally.
- The experience must feel design-forward and distinctly human, avoiding generic AI-generated wellness styling.

## Product Principles

1. Make recognition immediate: visitors should see their private experience reflected without being reduced to a diagnosis.
2. Show how Maya works: practical tools, emotional depth, pacing, safety, and client participation should be concrete throughout.
3. Use quiet confidence: every word, image, and interaction should reduce uncertainty and support trust.
4. Preserve truth over conversion tricks: no invented proof, urgency, or guaranteed outcomes.
5. Treat the office as part of the experience: calm, privacy, natural light, and in-person or telehealth choice deserve a meaningful place in the journey.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-accessible navigation and controls, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive image alternatives, and responsive type and spacing.
